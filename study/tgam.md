# TGAM — The Generative Art Museum (tgam.xyz)

Studied 2026-09-22 (Hans: study never finishes; this is a living source).

## The org
- Non-profit in Barcelona devoted to generative art / creative coding.
- Runs **Issue** exhibitions (Issue #09 "Continuum", Dec 2026–Mar 2025... [2024–2025]:
  Anna Lucia, Heeey, Jacek Markusiewicz). Continuum thesis: traditional and
  digital are one continuum, not opposing camps — shows blend embroidery,
  architecture, lighting with code.
- Runs **Responsive Dreams**, Barcelona's first festival fully dedicated to
  code-made art (since 2023; talks, live coding, DJ sets; special mention at
  Premis Ciutat de Barcelona).
- Open call **push(); 2026**: 3 projects, 16 weeks mentorship.
- Virtual gallery in Voxels. Newsletter, interviews, articles.

## What-is-generative-art: the canon they teach
Key principles: **algorithmic creation, autonomy, complexity/emergence**.
Precursors: Islamic girih tiling (decagonal quasi-patterns, Darb-e Imam),
Dada/Surrealist chance (Arp, Duchamp, Ernst), golden-ratio composition (Pacioli/da Vinci).
1950s: Ben Laposky Oscillons (oscilloscope waveforms on film), John Whitney
(analog computer films), John Cage (I Ching chance operations).
1960s: Georg Nees (23-Ecke, first algorithmic exhibition Stuttgart Feb 1965),
Frieder Nake, A. Michael Noll (Bell Labs), Ernest Edmonds, Sol LeWitt
(wall drawings = plain-English algorithms executed by drafters).
1970s: Harold Cohen AARON, Vera Molnar (Hypertransformations), Herbert Franke.
1980s–90s: William Latham/Stephen Todd (evolutionary organic forms), Manfred
Mohr (cube systems), Karl Sims (genetic images/creatures), John Maeda (MIT).

## Deep visual pass: Issue #09 "Continuum" artists (2026-09-22, works inspected at full res)

**Anna Lucia** (artist/engineer): vertical strip composition, ~50+ bars of
varying widths, each subdivided into stacked segments of differing heights.
Palette dominated by periwinkle/lavender with disciplined accents: orange,
gold, teal, deep navy, white. Thin dark dividers between strips. Reads as a
generative barcode or woven textile — systematic, rhythmic, data-like.
Seed: *data-barcode weavings* — column segments from a seeded distribution,
one accent color per column family.

**Heeey** (generative artist, minimalism): concentric rings in saturated neon
(lime, cyan, yellow, orange, red, magenta) with a dry-brush bristle-streak
texture along each ring, floating on blue. Hand-painted feel inside a strict
geometric system. Minimal but loud.
Seed: *dry-brush orbits* — concentric geometry rendered with streaky,
imperfect stroke texture; restraint in form, excess in color.

**Jacek Markusiewicz** (architect, creative coder): dramatic monochrome
3D terrain — smooth ribbon bands flowing through rugged noise-displaced
mountain strata, strong light/shadow sculpting. Architectural, geological.
Seed: *strata flow* — layered contour ribbons carved through a noise terrain,
grayscale, one light direction.

## Piece seeds for the doodles (study, never copy)
1. **Girih tiling** — decagonal quasi-crystalline star polygons, one accent glaze color.
2. **23-Ecke grid** — a grid of variations on one theme, each cell a small mutation.
3. **Molnar disorder** — strict geometric grid with ~1% random displacement/rotation.
4. **Mohr cube** — hypercube edges rotated through projection planes, monochrome.
5. **Oscillons** — layered Lissajous/waveform traces, phosphor-on-dark.
6. **LeWitt instructions** — lines from corners/sides/midpoints per a written recipe.
7. **Arp chance collage** — torn-paper rectangles dropped by chance, layered.
8. **Continuum bridge** — one piece in two media voices: e.g. embroidery-stitch
   rendering of a code pattern (stitch = dashed line work over a flow field).
