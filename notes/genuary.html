genuary.art | What it is: a month of daily generative art prompts, every January, one prompt per day, everyone invited
Depth
deep on the prompt archive. Visually inspected the site (home and prompts pages), read all 93 prompts across the 2024, 2025, and 2026 editions. Did not browse individual participant submissions; the value here is the prompt list itself as a technique bank.

Why it matters for this project

Genuary is the best prompt engine in generative art. Three years of daily constraints, written by working artists, covering almost every technique in the field. Instead of staring at a blank canvas, pick a prompt and let the constraint do the designing. Several of the doodle ideas in the pipeline will come straight from this list.

The site itself

Delightfully handmade. Black background, hot pink and cyan headers, a joke code block on the home page, a snarky privacy notice. No framework energy, just HTML with personality. A good reminder that the container can be plain as long as the content has teeth.

What makes it sing

The prompts are specific enough to start and open enough to surprise. "Design a rug" beats "make something with patterns" because it hands you a use, a shape, and a tradition. The yearly rhythm builds a public archive of thousands of answers to the same question, which is the fastest way to learn how differently one constraint can be read.

What is overdone

Some prompts recycle year to year (Bauhaus, shaders, SDF). The hashtag format rewards quick readable pieces over deep ones, so most submissions are sketches, not finished work. Fine for training, not a gallery standard.

How to use this for Generative Doodles

  • When stuck, pull a constraint prompt and time-box to one session. 2026.28

(HTML elements only) or 2025.27 (no randomness, no noise, no trig) are excellent warm-ups.

  • When learning, pull a technique prompt and study it properly: pixel sorting,

SDF, flocking, subdivision. One prompt, one deep dive, one piece.

  • The art-history prompts (Vera Molnar, Anni Albers, Bauhaus, Op Art) pair well

with the Gorillasun rule-extraction method: study the movement, extract the rules, generate new instances.

  • Plotter-ready (2026.22) is worth doing literally: design one piece that could

actually be plotted, single-weight lines, no fills.
